abstract: >-
  A real, topically relevant citation can still under-warrant the claim attached to it. We study this failure as citation laundering and introduce FORCEBENCH, a contrastive stress test for evidence-force calibration: each item holds a cited passage fixed and pairs an evidence-calibrated claim with a force-raised variant across relation, modality, scope, temporal validity, and numeric specificity. Generic support prompting is insufficient (aggregate monotonicity violation rate 47.2%); explicit warrant-strength prompting lowers it to 24.5% but remains imperfect.
